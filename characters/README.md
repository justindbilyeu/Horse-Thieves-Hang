# Character Portraits

## Purpose

Catalog the people who made the story. Not comprehensive biographies - just what's vivid and true.

## What to Capture

- **First impression** - What you saw/thought when you first met them
- **Physical details** - Specific things that stuck (scars, walk, hands, eyes)
- **Voice** - How they talked (rhythm, phrases, accent, verbal tics)
- **Key moments** - The scene(s) where they mattered most
- **Why they stick** - What made them memorable

## Guidelines

- Use real details, not generic descriptions
- Include dialogue if you remember how they talked
- Don't sanitize - capture who they actually were
- It's okay if you only remember fragments
- No need to tie it all together - just capture what's there
