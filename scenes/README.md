# Scenes

## Purpose

Inventory specific moments with sensory detail and emotional truth. Don't worry about order or how they fit together yet - just capture what you remember clearly.

## What Makes a Good Scene

- **Concrete** - Specific time, place, people
- **Sensory** - What you saw, heard, smelled, felt
- **Action or dialogue** - Something happened or was said
- **Stakes** - Why this moment mattered

## Guidelines

- Write what you remember, not what 'should' have happened
- Include dialogue if you remember it - even approximate
- Physical details matter (weather, light, sounds, temperature)
- Capture feeling without explaining it
- It's okay if it's just a fragment
