# Horse Thieves Hang

**A memoir of Tent City, fire academy, and finding humanity in Arizona State Prison**

## About

This is the story of a Gen-X white kid who went to Maricopa County Jail Tent City in the early 1990s, then to Arizona State Prison where he became a firefighter and GED teacher. It's about the diverse, crazy, deeply human characters encountered along the way - a white experience in a sea of color, crime, and connection.

## Repository Structure

- **characters/** - Individual portraits of people who made the story
- **scenes/** - Specific moments organized by location/period
- **chapters/** - Structured narrative (assembled from scenes)
- **research/** - Timeline, fact-checking, location details
- **notes/** - Working thoughts, questions, voice guidelines
- **drafts/** - Rough drafts (not version controlled)

## Writing Workflow

1. **Capture raw material** → Write character portraits and scenes
2. **Organize** → Use timeline and notes to find patterns
3. **Structure** → Assemble scenes into chapters
4. **Refine** → Edit, tighten, find the voice

## Guiding Principles

- Specific over general
- Show don't tell
- Voices matter - capture how people talked
- Truth over narrative convenience
- The story lives in the details
