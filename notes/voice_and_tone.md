# Voice and Tone

## Core Voice

[To be defined as you write - what does this story sound like?]

## What to Avoid
- Prison memoir clichés
- Over-explanation
- Redemption arc manipulation
- Softening the truth

## What to Embrace
- Specific detail
- Real voices/dialogue
- Contradictions
- Uncomfortable truths
- Humanity in unexpected places

## Questions
- First person? Third?
- Past or present tense?
- How much internal reflection vs. pure scene?
- How much context does the reader need?
